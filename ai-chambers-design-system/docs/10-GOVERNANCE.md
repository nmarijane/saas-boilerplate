# Governance

Token or contract changes require a versioned decision, migration note, component tests and updated light/dark visual baselines. Screens cannot invent their own padding, type, colour, radius or selection model. Name deliberate variants after a business use; do not add “newStyle2.”

Authority: approved business schema > DESIGN/tokens > UX-CONTRACT > component registry > examples > generated images. A mockup button is not proof of a feature. Never invent retention, authorisation, billing or legal policy to fill a screen.

Cards, switches and toasts are valid when useful. The prohibited patterns are meaningless decoration, marketing heroes inside workspaces, forced gamification, oversized display text, motion theatre and lost context. Prefer RecordTable/inspector for objects; optional grids retain queries/actions.

Versions: patch for compatible fixes; minor for approved new variants; major for changed public APIs or token meaning. Deprecations name the canonical replacement and removal plan. Avoid indefinite parallel UI systems.
